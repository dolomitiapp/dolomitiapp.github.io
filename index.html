Privacy Policy

Effective date: 26 September 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the mobile application Orari Dolomiti Bus (“the App”), developed by DolomitiApp.

1. Overview

Orari Dolomiti Bus enables users to save favourite public-transport stops and view publicly available public-transport arrival information.

The App does not require users to create an account and does not operate a developer-owned server for storing user accounts, favourite stops, or app preferences.

The App uses third-party services, including Google AdMob, to display advertisements.

2. Personal Data and Information Processing

The developer does not directly collect or maintain a database of users' personal information.

Favourite stops and app preferences are stored locally on the user's device and are not transmitted to servers operated by the developer.

However, the App uses third-party services that may process technical and advertising-related information as described below.

3. Favourite Stops and App Preferences

Favourite bus stops and related app preferences are stored only on the user's device.

This information is not transmitted to or stored on servers operated by DolomitiApp.

Users can delete locally stored information by removing favourite stops within the App, clearing the App's data through Android device settings, or uninstalling the App.

4. Public-Transport Arrival Information

The App retrieves public-transport arrival information from publicly available Südtirolmobil services and related public-transport information services.

When the App retrieves arrival information, network requests are made directly from the user's device to the relevant public-transport service.

DolomitiApp does not operate the public-transport service and does not receive or store the user's network request data.

The public-transport service provider may process technical information associated with requests, such as IP address, request information, timestamps, and server logs, according to its own privacy policy and data-processing practices.

5. Google AdMob

The App uses Google AdMob to display advertisements.

AdMob is a third-party advertising service provided by Google. When advertisements are displayed or requested, Google and its advertising partners may process certain information from or about the user's device.

Depending on the user's device settings, consent choices, applicable laws, and the advertising configuration, this information may include:

  • IP address;
  • advertising or other device identifiers;
  • device and operating-system information;
  • application and advertising information;
  • information about interactions with advertisements;
  • approximate location derived from network information; and
  • information used for advertising measurement, fraud prevention, and advertising delivery.

This information may be used to provide, measure, maintain, and improve advertising services, including personalized or non-personalized advertising where permitted.

DolomitiApp does not directly receive or maintain a database of this advertising information. Data processing by Google and its advertising partners is governed by their respective privacy policies and terms.

6. Advertising Consent

Where required by applicable law, the App may request the user's consent before personalized advertising is provided.

Users may also have controls through their device, Google account, or the advertising consent mechanism provided by the App.

For users in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, advertising-related processing is subject to applicable privacy and consent requirements.

The availability and type of advertising may depend on the user's consent and privacy choices.

7. Sharing of Data

DolomitiApp does not sell users' personal information.

DolomitiApp does not operate a server that stores users' favourite stops, app preferences, or user accounts.

The App does, however, use third-party services such as Google AdMob. Information may therefore be processed by Google and its advertising partners as necessary to provide advertising and related services.

Public-transport information requests are also sent directly from the user's device to the relevant public-transport service.

8. Data Security

Locally stored favourite stops and app preferences remain on the user's device and can be removed by the user.

The App uses HTTPS for network communication with services that support HTTPS.

Third-party service providers are responsible for the security and processing of information handled through their services.

9. Data Retention

DolomitiApp does not retain personal information in a developer-operated database.

Favourite stops and app preferences stored locally remain on the user's device until the user removes them, clears the App's data, or uninstalls the App.

Information processed by third-party services such as Google AdMob is retained and processed according to those providers' respective policies and retention practices.

10. Children's Privacy

The App does not require users to provide personal information or create an account.

The developer does not knowingly collect personal information directly from children.

Because the App displays advertisements through Google AdMob, advertising treatment and data processing for children or child-directed users are subject to applicable Google advertising policies and applicable privacy laws.

11. Third-Party Services

The App uses third-party services including:

Google AdMob
Used to display and measure advertisements.

Südtirolmobil / public-transport information services
Used to retrieve publicly available public-transport arrival information.

These third-party providers may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if the App's features, third-party services, or data-processing practices change.

The current version will be published on this page together with its effective date.
